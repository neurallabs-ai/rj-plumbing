Why does it matter that you do your own excavation?

There's no waiting on a second contractor. The same company digs, repairs the line and backfills, so the job finishes sooner.

Do you do leveling and hauling on their own?

Yes. Leveling and hauling are services on their own, as well as part of our plumbing and septic work.

Yes. We work across Winnipeg and around Starbuck, Oak Bluff and Headingley.
