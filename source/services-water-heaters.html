FAQ

Water Heater Repair & Replacement: Common Questions

Still unsure? Call and ask. You'll get a straight answer, any hour.

(204) 453-8386
Should I repair or replace my water heater?

If the tank itself is leaking, it needs replacing. If it's a part like a thermostat, element or valve, a repair is often worth it. We'll tell you which we'd choose in your position.

Can you replace a tank in a tight space?

Yes. Tight and awkward utility rooms are a regular job for us. One of our recent Google reviews is about exactly that.

My tank is leaking right now. What do I do?

Turn off the cold water supply to the tank and its power or gas, then call (204) 453-8386. We're open 24/7.
