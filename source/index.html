FAQ

Straight Answers Before You Call

Still unsure? Call and ask. You'll get a straight answer, any hour.

(204) 453-8386
Are you a licensed plumber in Winnipeg?

Yes. Robert Jacobs is listed with the City of Winnipeg as a licensed plumbing contractor under R. Jacobs Plumbing, and the company is a BBB Accredited Business with an A+ rating.

Are you open 24/7 for emergencies?

Yes. We're open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For anything urgent, call (204) 453-8386 instead of using the form. If water is spreading, shut off your main water valve while you wait.

Do you handle commercial plumbing?

Yes. We do residential and commercial work, from service calls to larger projects for businesses and general contractors.

Do you install sump pits and backwater valves?

Yes. Sump pits and backwater valves are one of the most effective ways to protect a Winnipeg basement from sewer backup during heavy rain and spring melt.

Do you work outside the city?

Yes. We serve Winnipeg from our Edderton Ave location, and Starbuck, Oak Bluff and Headingley from our Starbuck location, including septic tanks, fields and excavation.

How do I get an estimate?

Send the short estimate form with a quick description of the job, or call us. We'll talk through the problem and your options before any work starts.
