Septic 3 min read · September 12, 2026

Septic Care forRural Manitoba Homes

A septic field can last for decades, or fail early. Here's how rural homeowners around Starbuck, Oak Bluff and Headingley keep theirs healthy.

By the team at R. Jacobs Plumbing · City-licensed, BBB A+, serving Manitoba since 2007

Quick answer

Have the tank pumped regularly (often every 3 to 5 years, depending on tank size and household), keep wipes, grease and harsh chemicals out of the drains, spread out laundry, fix running toilets, and never drive or build on the field. Slow drains, sewage smells or soggy ground over the field mean it's time to call.

If you live on an acreage or farm around Starbuck, Oak Bluff or Headingley, your septic system is your sewer. Treat it well and it'll do its job quietly for a long time. Neglect it and replacing a field is one of the biggest bills a rural homeowner can face.

How a septic system works

Wastewater flows into the tank, where solids settle to the bottom and grease floats to the top. The liquid in the middle flows out to the field, where the soil filters it. The tank only works if the solids are pumped out before they build up and get pushed into the field.

How often to pump the tank

Many households need pumping every 3 to 5 years. Bigger families, smaller tanks and garburators all mean more often. If you don't know when it was last done, it's probably due.

What not to put down the drain

  • Wipes of any kind, including "flushable" ones.
  • Cooking grease and oil.
  • Paint, solvents and other harsh chemicals.
  • Medications.
  • Large amounts of bleach or antibacterial cleaners, which kill the bacteria the tank depends on.
  • Heavy garburator use. Food scraps add solids fast.

Protect the field

  • Don't drive, park or put buildings over the field or tank.
  • Keep trees and shrubs with big roots away from it.
  • Point downspouts and sump discharge away from the field so it isn't flooded.
  • Spread laundry out over the week instead of doing it all in one day.
  • Fix running toilets and dripping taps. Extra water overloads the field.

Septic care through a Manitoba winter

  • Leave snow cover over the field and tank alone. It insulates them.
  • Keep vehicles and snowmobiles off the field. Packed snow lets frost drive deeper.
  • Use water normally. A system that sits unused for weeks is more likely to freeze.
  • If you'll be away for a long stretch, ask us how to protect the system.

Warning signs

  • Slow drains throughout the house.
  • Gurgling in the plumbing.
  • Sewage smells outside, near the tank or field.
  • Soggy ground or unusually green grass over the field.
  • Sewage backing up into the lowest drains.

Sewage backing up into the house is an emergency. Stop using water and call (204) 453-8386. We're open 24/7.

Local septic service

We install and repair septic tanks and fields from our Starbuck location on Highway 2, and run our own excavation equipment, so one company handles the whole job. We serve Starbuck, Oak Bluff, Headingley and rural properties around Winnipeg.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a septic tank be pumped?

Many households need it every 3 to 5 years. Larger families, smaller tanks and garburator use mean more often.

Do septic additives help?

Most homes don't need them. Regular pumping and being careful about what goes down the drains matter far more.

Can you replace a failing septic field?

Yes. We handle the excavation, the new field and the backfill ourselves.
